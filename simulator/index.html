Your shots

    How this works.

    We keep the dose fixed at 18g and the machine on double shot, so the only thing you change is grind size. That's how you should dial in at home too: one change at a time.

    Finer grind = slower shot. Smaller grounds pack tighter, so water takes longer to push through. Coarser grind = faster shot. On this grinder, a smaller number is finer.

    The numbers here are a teaching model, not a recipe. Your beans, their roast and how fresh they are will move your sweet spot. Use the same process on your machine and you'll get there.
